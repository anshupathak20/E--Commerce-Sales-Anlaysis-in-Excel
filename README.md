# 🛒 E-Commerce Sales Analysis Using Excel

## 📌 Project Overview

This project is an **E-Commerce Sales Data Analysis project created entirely in Microsoft Excel**.

The objective of this project is to analyze e-commerce sales data and identify important business patterns related to **sales performance, customers, products, sales channels, order status, and geographic markets**.

Using Excel's data analysis and visualization capabilities, the raw transaction data was transformed into meaningful **KPIs, Pivot Tables, charts, dashboards, and business insights**.

---

## 🎯 Business Objective

The main objective is to understand:

* Overall sales performance
* Customer purchasing behavior
* Top-performing product categories
* Best-performing sales channels
* State-wise sales performance
* Monthly sales trends
* Order status distribution
* Customer demographic performance
* Opportunities for business growth

The analysis is designed from a **business decision-making perspective**, rather than just presenting numbers.

# 🔍 Key Business Insights

## 👩 1. Female Customers Generate More Revenue

Female customers generated approximately **₹13.56M**, compared with approximately **₹7.61M** from male customers.

Female customers therefore contribute around **64% of total sales**.

### Business Insight

The business has a strong female customer base.

The company can focus on:

* Women's product promotions
* Personalized offers
* Customer loyalty campaigns
* Product recommendations
* Targeted digital marketing

---

# 👗 2. Sets Are the Top-Performing Category

Sales by major category:

| Category      |   Sales |
| ------------- | ------: |
| Set           | ₹10.51M |
| Kurta         |  ₹4.96M |
| Western Dress |  ₹3.15M |
| Top           |  ₹1.19M |
| Saree         |  ₹1.01M |

The **Set category is the largest contributor to revenue**.

### Business Insight

The company should consider increasing:

* Inventory for high-performing Sets
* Product variety
* Promotional campaigns

# 🚚 7. Delivered Orders Dominate the Order Status

The majority of transactions are successfully delivered.

However, the dataset also contains:

* Returned orders
* Cancelled orders

### Business Insight

* Category
* SKU

* Customer segment

# 💡 Key Business Recommendations

### 1. Strengthen the Women's Segment

Female customers generate the majority of revenue, so the company should continue investing in women's product marketing and customer retention.

### 2. Prioritize the Set Category

Sets are the largest revenue contributor. Inventory, product launches and promotional campaigns can be prioritized around this category.

### 3. Maintain Strong Amazon Presence

Amazon is the highest-performing channel, making it an important platform for inventory and marketing investment.

### 4. Focus on High-Value States

Maharashtra, Karnataka, Uttar Pradesh, Telangana and Tamil Nadu should be treated as priority markets.

### 5. Investigate Returns

A detailed return analysis by product, size, category and channel could help reduce revenue loss.

### 6. Explore B2B Growth

The relatively small B2B contribution suggests an opportunity to develop a dedicated B2B/wholesale strategy.
